# Introduction

FiveOh Reviews on Metaobjects is the display layer for your product reviews. Collect reviews with FiveOh or keep the app you already use — either way, FiveOh stores them as native Shopify metaobjects and renders them server-side in your theme.

These docs follow the three things you do with reviews:

1. **[Collecting reviews](/docs/collecting-reviews)** — import from Judge.me, Loox, Reviews.io, Trustpilot or the Shop app, or collect natively with storefront forms and post-purchase email.
2. **[Managing reviews](/docs/managing-reviews)** — moderate, reply, feature, verify, add rating attributes, and auto-translate.
3. **[Displaying reviews](/docs/displaying-reviews)** — place and customise the storefront app blocks, down to the individual placeholder.

## Start here

1. Open the app in Shopify admin.
2. Connect at least one review source.
3. Enable and place your app blocks in the Theme Editor.
4. Confirm reviews are visible on storefront pages.
