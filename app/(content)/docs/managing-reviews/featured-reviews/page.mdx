# Featured reviews

Feature (pin) a review to surface it above the rest.

## How it works

- Mark a review as featured from the app dashboard.
- Featured reviews populate the `{featured_reviews}` region on the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list) and [Reviews Slider](/docs/displaying-reviews/reviews-slider).
- Each featured review can also show a `{featured_badge}` on the item itself.

## Displaying it

Keep `{featured_reviews}` in the block structure to render the pinned region, and `{featured_badge}` in the review-item structure for the badge. See [Customising app blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-app-blocks).

## Troubleshooting

- If featured reviews do not appear, confirm at least one review is marked featured and `{featured_reviews}` is in the structure.
