# Managing reviews

Once reviews are in your store, you manage them in the reviews table on the app dashboard. Every row is a review metaobject, and every change you make there is written to Shopify and shows on your storefront within moments.

1. **[Moderation](#moderation):** decide which reviews are published.
2. **[Replies](/docs/managing-reviews/replies):** answer a review publicly.
3. **[Featured reviews](/docs/managing-reviews/featured-reviews):** pin your best reviews to the top.
4. **[Verified badge](/docs/managing-reviews/verified-badge):** show which reviews come from real buyers.
5. **[Additional attributes](/docs/managing-reviews/additional-attributes):** show ratings for fit, quality or comfort.
6. **[Translations](/docs/managing-reviews/translations):** reviews in every language of your store.
7. **[Admin blocks](/docs/managing-reviews/admin-blocks):** a product's, customer's or order's reviews right on its page in the Shopify admin.

## Moderation

Every review is either **active**, which means published, or a **draft**, which is hidden from the storefront. Change a review's status in the reviews table to publish or hide it. Ratings, counts and review lists are recalculated right away, so a hidden review no longer counts anywhere.

Reviews written in your [review form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) or [post-purchase form](/docs/displaying-reviews/post-purchase-form) are published at once, unless they match one of your moderation rules. In the app settings, you can keep reviews as drafts when they:

1. **Have a certain verification status,** for example reviews from visitors who are not verified buyers.
2. **Contain photos or videos, or a link.** Links are a common sign of spam.
3. **Have a certain rating,** for example 1 or 2 stars, so you can respond before they go live.

Drafts wait in the reviews table until you publish them.

## Troubleshooting

1. **A change does not show on the storefront:** wait a moment and reload. Theme caches can delay it by a minute.
2. **A new review does not appear:** it may be a draft because of a moderation rule.
