# Managing reviews

Once reviews are in your store, you manage them from the reviews table in the app dashboard. Each row is a review metaobject you can moderate and enrich.

## What you can do

- **[Moderate](#moderation)** — approve or hide reviews and control what shows on the storefront.
- **[Reply](/docs/managing-reviews/replies)** — publish a merchant response under a review.
- **[Feature](/docs/managing-reviews/featured-reviews)** — pin your best reviews to the top.
- **[Verified badge](/docs/managing-reviews/verified-badge)** — mark reviews backed by a real order.
- **[Additional attributes](/docs/managing-reviews/additional-attributes)** — collect extra rating dimensions like fit or quality.
- **[Translations](/docs/managing-reviews/translations)** — auto-translate reviews into your store languages.

## Moderation

Each review has a status that controls storefront visibility. Change a review's status from the reviews table to publish or hide it. The status also drives the `{status}` placeholder rendered on each review — see [Customising app blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-app-blocks).

## Troubleshooting

- If a change is not visible on the storefront, wait for sync to complete, then reload the page.
- If a review will not publish, check its status and that its product link resolved.
