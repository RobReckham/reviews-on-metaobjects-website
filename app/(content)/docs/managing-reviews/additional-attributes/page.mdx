# Additional attributes

Beyond the overall star rating, you can collect extra **slider ratings** — dimensions like fit, quality or comfort — configured on the **Additional attributes** page in the app.

## Defining an attribute

Each attribute is a slider with:

- **Question** — what the customer answers on the review form (e.g. "How was the fit?").
- **Label** — a short label shown in review lists (e.g. "Fit").
- **Left / Center / Right labels** — the slider's endpoints and midpoint (e.g. Runs small · True to size · Runs large).
- **Scale min / max** — the numeric range of the slider.

Attributes are scoped to products.

## Collection & display

- The **question** appears as a slider input on the [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) and [Post Purchase Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/post-purchase-form).
- Submitted values render on each review as a labelled slider, and aggregate across the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list).

## Troubleshooting

- If an attribute is not asked on the form, confirm it is saved and scoped to products.
- If older reviews show no value, they predate the attribute — only reviews collected afterwards carry it.
