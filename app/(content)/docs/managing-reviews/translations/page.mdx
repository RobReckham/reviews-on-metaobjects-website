# Translations

FiveOh translates every review into the languages of your store, so a shopper on your French storefront reads French reviews. Nothing needs to be set up or placed: once your store has more than one language, reviews follow the storefront language.

## How it works

1. **When a review comes in,** FiveOh detects its language and translates its title, text and your reply into every language you added to your store.
2. **The translations are stored in Shopify,** as translations of the review's `review_meta` metaobject. Liquid returns the shopper's language automatically, in our blocks and in your own theme code.
3. **When the text changes,** for example when the reviewer edits it or you add a reply, the review is translated again. Otherwise its translations stay as they are.

The original text is always kept on the review's `product_review`.

A language you add to your store later is used for new reviews and for reviews whose text changes. Existing reviews are not translated into it on their own.

## Search follows the language

The search in the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list) looks in the language of the storefront. On the English storefront, "comfortable" finds reviews that say so in their English translation, including reviews written in German.

## Block texts

The texts of the blocks themselves, like headings and button labels, are not translated automatically. Translate them in the **Translate & Adapt** app, see [Texts in other languages](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-blocks#texts-in-other-languages).

## Troubleshooting

1. **A review is not translated into a language:** check that the language is added in your store's language settings. Reviews that existed before the language was added are not translated into it.
2. **Block texts stay in English:** translate the block's **Texts** setting in Translate & Adapt.
