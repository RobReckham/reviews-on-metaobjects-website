# Translations

Reviews are translated automatically into your store's languages, so shoppers read them in their own locale.

## How it works

- When a review is added or updated, its title and body are translated into each of your store's **published locales**.
- The source language is detected automatically.
- Translations are stored on the review and registered as Shopify translation content, so display follows the shopper's locale through Shopify's native translation system — no widget, still server-rendered.

## What you control

- Your store's published languages determine the target locales. Add a language in Shopify and new reviews are translated into it.
- Nothing to place on the storefront: the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list) and other blocks render the locale-appropriate text automatically.

## Troubleshooting

- If a review is not translated into a language, confirm that language is **published** in your Shopify markets/languages settings.
- Translations apply going forward; re-sync a source if you need older reviews reprocessed.
