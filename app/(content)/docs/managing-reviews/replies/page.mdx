# Replies

Respond to a review publicly with a merchant reply — useful for thanking customers or addressing a concern.

## How it works

- Add or edit a reply on a review from the app dashboard.
- The reply is stored on the review and syncs to its Shopify metaobject.
- It renders through the `{reply}` placeholder wherever the review appears.

## Displaying it

Keep `{reply}` in your review-item structure. When a review has no reply, the token renders nothing. See [Customising app blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-app-blocks#review-item-each-review-inside-the-list).

## Troubleshooting

- If a reply does not show, confirm it saved and that `{reply}` is present in the review-item structure.
