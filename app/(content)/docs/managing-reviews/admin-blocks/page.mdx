# Admin blocks

FiveOh's admin blocks show reviews where you already work: on the product, variant, customer and order pages of your Shopify admin. You see at a glance how a product is rated, what a customer has written, or whether an order was reviewed, without opening the app.

| Page | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Rating, distribution and the newest reviews of the product, including its variants |
| **Variant** | Rating, distribution and the newest reviews of the variant |
| **Customer** | Rating, distribution and the newest reviews the customer wrote |
| **Order** | Rating, distribution and the newest reviews of the order, plus its review request: a link to the entry in your admin and a link to its review page on your storefront |

Each review shows whether it is published or a draft. **Manage reviews in FiveOh** opens the app.

## Adding them

Admin blocks are added once per page type. Open a product in your Shopify admin, find the app blocks area of the page and add **FiveOh Reviews on Metaobjects**. Shopify then shows the block on every product. Do the same on a variant, a customer and an order.

## Troubleshooting

1. **The block shows no reviews:** the product, customer or order has none yet, or they are drafts.
2. **The order block has no review request link:** no review request was created for the order, for example because the post-purchase email is turned off.
