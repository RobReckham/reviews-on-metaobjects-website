# Trust signals

Trust signals are five small blocks that each show one line of proof, like `120+ sold in the last 7 days` or `95% 5-star reviews`.

| Block | Shows | Example |
|---|---|---|
| [Bestseller](#bestseller) | The product's sales rank | `#2 best-selling in the last 30 days` |
| [Sold Count](#sold-count) | Units sold | `120+ sold in the last 7 days` |
| [Last Purchased](#last-purchased) | Time since the last purchase | `Last purchased 6 hours ago` |
| [Returning Customers](#returning-customers) | Share of buyers who came back | `38% are returning customers` |
| [Rating Share](#rating-share) | Share of reviews at a star level | `95% 5-star reviews` |

The first four read the **live signals** FiveOh calculates from your orders, for the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days and all time. Rating Share reads the review distribution. On product pages, every signal uses the product's figures; elsewhere, the store's, except Bestseller, which only works on product pages.

## Where to place it

1. Product page, around the add-to-cart button

## Adding a signal

1. In the Theme Editor, open a product template.
2. In the section with the add-to-cart button, click **Add block** and choose the signal from the **Apps** tab, for example **FiveOh Sold Count**.
3. Set its period and threshold, then **update its text to match**: the text is not changed for you when you pick another period.

Each signal hides itself when there is no data, or when the figure is outside the thresholds you set. So you can place a signal on every product and it only shows where it is worth showing.

Each block has one text in its **Texts** setting, with the values listed below. Change, extend and translate it like any block text, see [Customising blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-blocks#texts).

## Bestseller

Shows the product's rank by units sold in a period, among all products or within collections you pick. Only on product pages.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Period** | Last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days (default) or all time. |
| **Rank within collections** | Up to 50 collections, in order. Shows the rank within the first of them the product is in, like `Women` before `Men`. Empty by default: the rank among all products. |
| **Maximum rank to show** | Shows the block only for the top products, `10` by default. Ranks within collections go up to 50. |

| Key | Default | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `bestseller` | `#{a} best-selling in the last 30 days` | `{a}` rank, `{b}` title of the collection the rank is in (empty without collections) |

Ranks change with sales, so a text like `Top {a} this month` or simply `Bestseller` works too. With collections, name the collection: `#{a} in {b}`.

## Sold Count

Shows how many units were sold in a period.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Period** | Last 24 hours, 7 days (default), 30 days or all time. |
| **Minimum count to show** | Hides the block below this number, `1` by default. |

| Key | Default | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `sold_count` | `{a}+ sold in the last 7 days` | `{a}` units sold, `{b}` the same, short (`12.9k`) |

## Last Purchased

Shows how long ago something was last bought, like `4 days ago`, in the shopper's language. On product pages it is the product or the selected variant; elsewhere the store's last purchase. The relative time is calculated in the browser, so it needs the [FiveOh Core](/docs/displaying-reviews/core) app embed.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Maximum age to show** | Hides the block when the purchase is older: 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days (default) or 30 days, or always show. |

| Key | Default | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `last_purchased` | `Last purchased {a}` | `{a}` relative time, `{b}` relative time for the selected variant, `{c}` date, `{d}` date for the selected variant |

## Returning Customers

Shows the share of buyers in a period who had ordered from your store before. On product pages, the buyers of that product.

This measures customers returning to your **store**, not customers buying the same product again. Word the text accordingly.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Period** | Last 24 hours, 7 days (default), 30 days or all time. |
| **Minimum percent to show**, **Minimum count to show** | Hide the block below these. `0` ignores them. |

| Key | Default | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `returning_customers` | `{a}% are returning customers` | `{a}` percent, `{b}` number of returning customers |

## Rating Share

Shows the share of reviews at a star level, from the product's reviews on product pages and from all reviews elsewhere.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Share type** | 5 stars (default), 4 stars and more, 2 stars and less, or 1 star. |
| **Minimum percent to show**, **Minimum count to show** | Hide the block below these. `0` ignores them. |
| **Maximum percent to show**, **Maximum count to show** | Hide the block above these. `100` and `0` ignore them. |

| Key | Default | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `rating_share` | `{a}% 5-star reviews` | `{a}` percent, `{b}` number of reviews |

The maximums are for the negative share types: with **1 star** and a **Maximum percent to show** of `5`, a text like `Only {a}% 1-star reviews` shows only while it is true.

## Data

The block reads these metafields. Your theme code can read the same ones; their full structure is in the [reference](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields). On product pages they are the product's, elsewhere the shop's.

1. [`live_signals`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#live_signals): units sold, sales rank and returning customers for four periods, and the time of the last purchase. Bestseller, Sold Count, Last Purchased and Returning Customers read it. Bestseller with collections reads the collections' `live_signals` instead, which hold the rank within each collection.
2. [`rating_distribution`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#rating_distribution): the number and share of reviews per star. Rating Share reads it.

## Examples

Every value these blocks use is in the `live_signals` and `rating_distribution` metafields of the product, the shop and your collections, so you can show signals anywhere in your theme. Their structure is in the [Data](#data) section above.

The examples are one line of text each, so they need no CSS: they take the font and color of the spot in your theme where you put them.

### Units sold in the last 24 hours

Shown only from five units, a higher minimum than the block's default of 1:

<Example>
<p>12 sold in the last 24 hours</p>

```liquid
{%- assign fiveoh = product.metafields['app--324569563137'] -%}
{%- assign day = fiveoh.live_signals.value.periods.last_24_hours -%}
{%- if day.sold_count >= 5 -%}
  <p>{{ day.sold_count }} sold in the last 24 hours</p>
{%- endif -%}
```

</Example>

### Bestseller rank

`sold_rank` is the product's place among all products by units sold, here in the last 30 days, shown for the top 10:

<Example>
<p><strong>#2</strong> best-selling in the last 30 days</p>

```liquid
{%- assign month = product.metafields['app--324569563137'].live_signals.value.periods.last_30_days -%}
{%- if month.sold_rank and month.sold_rank <= 10 -%}
  <p><strong>#{{ month.sold_rank }}</strong> best-selling in the last 30 days</p>
{%- endif -%}
```

</Example>

### Bestseller rank within a collection

`sold_ranks` on a collection holds each product's place within that collection. Its keys are product IDs as text, so `product.id` gets `append: ''`. Here the place in the Shirts collection in the last 30 days, shown for the top 10:

<Example>
<p><strong>#1</strong> best-selling in Shirts</p>

```liquid
{%- assign shirts = collections['shirts'] -%}
{%- assign product_key = product.id | append: '' -%}
{%- assign place = shirts.metafields['app--324569563137'].live_signals.value.periods.last_30_days.sold_ranks[product_key] -%}
{%- if place and place <= 10 -%}
  <p><strong>#{{ place }}</strong> best-selling in {{ shirts.title }}</p>
{%- endif -%}
```

</Example>

### Share of 5-star reviews

`rating_distribution` holds the count and percent per star. Its keys are strings, so `'5'` is quoted:

<Example>
<p>87% of reviews give 5 stars</p>

```liquid
{%- assign five = product.metafields['app--324569563137'].rating_distribution.value['5'] -%}
{%- if five.percent >= 80 -%}
  <p>{{ five.percent | round }}% of reviews give 5 stars</p>
{%- endif -%}
```

</Example>

## Troubleshooting

1. **A signal does not show:** the figure is outside its thresholds, or there were no orders in the period. Try a longer period or a lower minimum.
2. **The text names the wrong period:** the text does not follow the **Period** setting. Update it by hand.
3. **Last Purchased shows a date in the wrong language, or nothing:** turn on the FiveOh Core app embed.
