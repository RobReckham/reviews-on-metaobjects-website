# Rating Share

Displays the share of reviews at a chosen rating level, e.g. *"95% 5-star reviews"*.

## When to use

A compact trust signal near the product title, or on landing pages, when your rating distribution is strong.

## Settings

- **Share type** — 5 star / 4+ star / 2− star / 1 star.
- **Text** — your copy, using `{share_percent}` and `{share_count}`. Default: `{share_percent}% 5-star reviews`.
- **Minimum percent / count to show** — hide below these; set to `0` to ignore.
- **Maximum percent / count to show** — hide above these; set percent to `100` to ignore.
- **Custom CSS** — under advanced settings.

Update the text when you change the share type (e.g. `Only {share_percent}% are 1-star`).

## Troubleshooting

- If nothing renders, the share is outside your minimum/maximum thresholds, or there are no reviews yet.
