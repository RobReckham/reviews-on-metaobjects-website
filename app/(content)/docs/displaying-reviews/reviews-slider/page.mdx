# Reviews Slider

The **FiveOh Reviews Slider** shows reviews as a row of cards that shoppers swipe or scroll through, led by a summary tile with your rating, the review count and an optional logo.

Unlike the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list), the slider has no search, filters or load more. It shows a fixed number of reviews, featured ones first if you like, then the newest.

## Where to place it

1. Homepage
2. Landing pages
3. Product page, in the main content

## Adding it to your theme

1. In the Theme Editor, open the template, for example the homepage.
2. Click **Add block** in a section and choose **FiveOh Reviews Slider** from the **Apps** tab.
3. Choose the **Review scope**: **mixed** for all reviews (the default), **store** for reviews about your shop, or **product** on product pages.

The previous and next buttons need the [FiveOh Core](/docs/displaying-reviews/core) app embed. Without it, the slider still scrolls by swiping.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Max reviews to show** | How many reviews the slider holds. |
| **Show first N featured reviews** | How many [featured reviews](/docs/managing-reviews/featured-reviews) come first. `0` shows only the newest. |
| Star icons | Your own star images. The stars take the text color of the section. |
| **Hide review information link** | Hides the "About these reviews" link. |
| **Summary logo** | An image in the summary tile, for example your brand or a review seal. |
| **Minimum reviews to show**, **Fallback behavior**, **Review scope** | Which reviews the slider shows, and when it hides. See [Customising blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-blocks#settings). |
| **Heading tag** | The HTML tag of the heading. |
| **Anchor ID** | An ID other elements can link to. |

## Data

The block reads these metafields. Your theme code can read the same ones; their full structure is in the [reference](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields). On product pages they are the product's, elsewhere the shop's.

1. [`newest_reviews`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#newest_reviews) and `newest_reviews_meta`: the newest reviews and their translations.
2. [`featured_reviews`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#featured_reviews) and `featured_reviews_meta`: the featured reviews, shown first.
3. [`rating`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#rating) and [`rating_count`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#rating_count): the summary tile.

Each review in the lists is a [`product_review`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#product_review) with its [`review_meta`](/docs/developers/metaobjects-and-metafields#review_meta).

## Examples

The slider reads the `newest_reviews` and `featured_reviews` lists of the product or the shop. Its stars follow the text color; to give them their own color, set `--fo-star-color: #e7a427;` in the block's **Custom CSS**.

The example below builds a slider in your own theme code. Each example brings the CSS for exactly the look of its preview, scoped to its own classes so it does not touch the rest of your theme. Only the font and the text color come from your theme. The stars (`fo-stars`) need FiveOh's star styles on the page, which every FiveOh block and the [Product Card Ratings](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-card-ratings) app embed load.

### A slider of featured reviews

`<fo-slider>` adds previous and next buttons to any scroll container marked with `data-fo-track`. The buttons are disabled at the start and the end. The cards are yours to design. The track scrolls without JavaScript; the buttons need the script, which the [FiveOh Core](/docs/displaying-reviews/core) app embed loads.

## Troubleshooting

1. **The slider is empty:** check that there are published reviews for the chosen **Review scope**.
2. **It shows store-wide reviews on a product page:** the scope is **mixed** or **store**. Set it to **product**.
3. **The buttons do nothing:** turn on the FiveOh Core app embed.
