# Returning Customers

Displays the share of returning customers for a period, e.g. *"38% are returning customers"*.

## When to use

A store-level trust signal for the homepage, footer, or landing pages.

## Settings

- **Period** — Last 24 hours / 7 days / 30 days / All time.
- **Text** — your copy, using `{return_rate}` and `{return_count}`. Default: `{return_rate}% are returning customers`.
- **Minimum percent to show** / **Minimum count to show** — hide below these; set to `0` to ignore.
- **Custom CSS** — under advanced settings.

> This is **shop-repeat** data. Word it as customers returning to your store — do not claim product repurchase.

## Troubleshooting

- If nothing renders, the rate or count is below your minimums, or there is not enough order history yet.
