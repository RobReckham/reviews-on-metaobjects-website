# FiveOh Core

**FiveOh Core** is an app embed, not a block: you turn it on once and it runs on every page. Enable it before placing any block, under **Theme Editor → App embeds**.

It does three things:

1. **Loads the FiveOh script,** which makes the interactive parts work: load more, sort, filter and search in the review list, the review forms, and the slider buttons. The script is small and loads deferred, after the page content.
2. **Adds your reviews to the structured data** of each product page, so search engines can show stars in their results and AI tools can read the rating.
3. **Keeps review request pages out of search engines.** The pages customers open from review request emails are personal and should not be indexed.

Everything that is pure Liquid, like ratings and the reviews shown on page load, renders without FiveOh Core. Interaction does not.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stop review requests from being indexed by search engines** | Adds `noindex` to the [post-purchase form](/docs/displaying-reviews/post-purchase-form) page. Keep it on. |
| **Show product rating and reviews in search results** | Adds the rating and the newest reviews to the product's structured data. Only use it when the rating is visible on the product page, as Google requires. |
| **Structured data product ID** | The `@id` of your theme's product markup, so Google merges price, rating and reviews into one product. The default, `{product_url}#product`, matches Shopify's own markup. Placeholders: `{product_url}`, `{shop_url}`, `{handle}`, `{product_id}`, `{variant_id}`, `{sku}`. |
| **Structured data product type** | `Product` or `ProductGroup`. **Auto** uses `ProductGroup` for products with variants, like Shopify does. |
| **Reviews in structured data** | How many of the newest reviews go into the structured data. Keep it at or below the [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list)'s **Preloaded reviews**, so every review in the structured data is also on the page. |
| **Custom CSS** | CSS for the whole storefront, not scoped to a block. |

## Structured data

FiveOh's structured data is the only review markup your product page needs. If your theme or another app also outputs a rating for the product, turn one of them off, otherwise Google sees two ratings for the same product.

To check the result, open a product in Google's <a href="https://search.google.com/test/rich-results" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Rich Results Test</a>. It should show one product with its rating and reviews.
