# Displaying reviews

App blocks control how reviews appear across your storefront. Every block renders server-side in Liquid — no JavaScript widget — and reads from your review metaobjects.

## Placement from the app

In Shopify admin, open **App blocks** in the app and use **Open in Theme Editor** for the block you want to place.

## Recommended setup order

1. Enable the [FiveOh Core](/docs/displaying-reviews/core) app embed.
2. Add [Product Rating](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-rating) and [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list) on product pages.
3. Add [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) where customers can submit feedback.
4. Enable [Product Card Ratings](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-card-ratings) for rating hints on listing pages.
5. Add a [Rating Bar](/docs/displaying-reviews/rating-bar) or [Reviews Slider](/docs/displaying-reviews/reviews-slider) for homepage and landing-page trust.

## The blocks

**Ratings & summary**
- [Product Rating](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-rating) — average stars and count for a product
- [Product Card Ratings](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-card-ratings) — compact stars for collection/listing cards
- [Rating Bar](/docs/displaying-reviews/rating-bar) — store- or product-level rating bar with logo

**Review display**
- [Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list) — the full list with search, sort, filters and pagination
- [Reviews Slider](/docs/displaying-reviews/reviews-slider) — featured reviews in a carousel

**Collection forms** *(placed as blocks; see [Collecting reviews](/docs/collecting-reviews))*
- [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) — on-store submission form
- [Post Purchase Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/post-purchase-form) — hosted form for email/link submissions

**Purchase signals**
- [Bestseller](/docs/displaying-reviews/bestseller) · [Sold Count](/docs/displaying-reviews/sold-count) · [Last Purchased](/docs/displaying-reviews/last-purchased) · [Returning Customers](/docs/displaying-reviews/returning-customers) · [Rating Share](/docs/displaying-reviews/rating-share) · [Floating Badge](/docs/displaying-reviews/floating-badge)

## Customising output

Every block builds its markup from a **structure** template with `{placeholder}` tokens, plus scoped `custom_css`. See [Customising app blocks](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-app-blocks) for the complete token reference.

## Troubleshooting

- If a block does not render, confirm it is enabled and saved in Theme Editor.
- If layout looks wrong, verify placement in the correct template and section.
- For card, form and purchase-signal blocks, check their dedicated pages for required data or integration steps.
