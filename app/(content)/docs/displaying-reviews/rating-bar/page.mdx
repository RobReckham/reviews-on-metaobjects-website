# Rating Bar

A horizontal rating bar showing average stars, rating and review count — optionally with a logo and a full-width background.

## When to use

A trust strip for the homepage, landing pages, or above the fold on product pages.

## Settings

- **Full-width background** — extend the background edge to edge.
- **Colors** — background, text and star colors.
- **Stars** — star size, and optional custom full/empty star icons.
- **Logo** — image and max width (e.g. a review-source or brand logo).
- **Review scope** — store-level or the current product.
- **Selector to scroll to on click** / **Link URL** — jump to your reviews block, or link elsewhere.

## Placeholders

`{stars}` `{rating}` `{count}` `{pluralised_reviews}` `{logo}` — see the [token reference](/docs/displaying-reviews/customising-app-blocks).

## Troubleshooting

- If no rating appears, confirm there is review data for the chosen **Review scope**.
- If click-to-scroll does nothing, re-check the target selector against your reviews block.
