# Installation

This page takes you from installing the app to reviews on your storefront. It takes about ten minutes, most of it spent waiting for an import.

## Install the app

Install **FiveOh Reviews on Metaobjects** from the <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/reviews-on-metaobjects" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Shopify App Store</a>. During the installation, FiveOh creates its metaobject and metafield definitions in your store. You do not need to set anything up in Shopify yourself.

## A tour of the app

You find FiveOh in your Shopify admin under **Apps**. Its menu has five pages:

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Reviews** | The dashboard. At the top, a card for every review source, with **Add platform** to connect a new one and buttons to sync it. Below, the reviews table, where you publish, hide, reply to and feature reviews. |
| **App Blocks** | Every storefront block, each with **Open in Theme Editor** to place it in your theme. |
| **Additional attributes** | Rating questions like fit or quality, shown with the reviews. |
| **Settings** | Moderation rules, the review request email, reward discount codes and AI summaries. |
| **Subscription** | Your plan. |

## Add your reviews

Next, bring in the reviews you already have. How depends on where they are today:

1. **Judge.me, Loox or the Shop app:** nothing to do. These apps store their reviews in Shopify already, and FiveOh picks them up on its own; a card for the source appears on the dashboard.
2. **REVIEWS.io or Trustpilot:** click **Add platform** on the dashboard, choose the source and enter its API credentials. Then run a sync.
3. **A CSV file** from any other app: on the dashboard's **File Import** card, click **Import file!** and upload it. The card also offers an example CSV.

The [Collecting reviews](/docs/collecting-reviews) overview compares the sources. Every imported review becomes a `product_review` metaobject in your store.

Once the import has finished, FiveOh calculates the ratings, review lists and summaries for every product. On large catalogues this runs in the background for a few minutes.

Starting from scratch? Skip the import and collect your own reviews: turn on the [post-purchase email](/docs/collecting-reviews/post-purchase-email), place the [Post Purchase Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/post-purchase-form) block that the email links to, and add the [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) to your product pages.

## Enable FiveOh Core

App embeds are switches in the Theme Editor that load an app's code on every page of your store, without a visible block. FiveOh Core is one: it loads the small script behind load more, sorting, filtering and the review forms, and adds your reviews to the product's structured data for search engines.

On the **App Blocks** page, click **Open in Theme Editor** next to FiveOh Core. The Theme Editor opens with the embed turned on; click **Save**. See [FiveOh Core](/docs/displaying-reviews/core).

## Place your first blocks

On the **App Blocks** page, **Open in Theme Editor** opens the right template and adds the block for you. Move it where you want it and click **Save**. A good first setup:

1. **[Product Rating](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-rating)** near the product title.
2. **[Review List](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-list)** further down the product page.
3. **[Product Card Ratings](/docs/displaying-reviews/product-card-ratings)** for stars in collection grids. Turn on its app embed, then add a small snippet to your theme's product card. That step needs a few lines of theme code, so you may want your developer for it.

Every block works with its defaults, so you can adjust colors and texts later.

## Check your storefront

Open a product that has reviews. You should see the rating and the review list. To confirm the reviews are rendered on the server, open the page source and search for a sentence from one of the reviews: it is already in the HTML.

If something is missing, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
