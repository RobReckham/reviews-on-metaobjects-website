# Loox

Loox stores its reviews in Shopify's standard product review metaobjects. FiveOh reads them from there, so you can keep collecting with Loox and display the reviews with FiveOh.

## Setup

There is nothing to set up. As soon as Loox has stored a review in Shopify, FiveOh notices it, adds it to your reviews and recalculates the product's rating. A **Loox** card appears on the <a href={`${process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ADMIN_URL}`} target="_blank" rel="noopener">app dashboard</a> once the first review arrives.

New, changed and deleted reviews follow on their own: Shopify tells FiveOh about every change.

## Checking that it works

Open the reviews table on the dashboard. The Loox reviews are listed there, with Loox as their source. Then open a product with Loox reviews on your storefront: the FiveOh blocks show them.

## Troubleshooting

1. **No reviews arrive:** in your Shopify admin, open **Content → Metaobjects → Product reviews**. If the Loox reviews are not listed there, Loox has not stored them in Shopify yet. Check Loox's settings for syncing reviews to Shopify.
2. **A review shows without a product:** Loox stored it without a product link. It counts as a store review.
