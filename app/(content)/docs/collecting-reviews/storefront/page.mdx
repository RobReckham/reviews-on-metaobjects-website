# Review form

Shoppers can write a review right on a product page, with stars, text, photos and videos. You add this with the **FiveOh Review Form** block; its settings and texts are described on the [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) block page.

## Setting it up

1. Place the [Review Form](/docs/displaying-reviews/review-form) block on your product template.
2. Choose **Who can review**: everyone, only logged-in customers, or only customers who bought the product.
3. Decide in the app's **Settings** whether new reviews are published at once or wait for your approval. See [Moderation](/docs/managing-reviews#moderation).

## What happens to a review

A review sent from the form is stored like every other review and shows in the reviews table on the dashboard. Reviews from logged-in customers are marked **Verified Reviewer**, reviews from visitors who are not logged in **Unverified**. To get **Verified Buyer** reviews, which are linked to an order, use the [post-purchase email](/docs/collecting-reviews/post-purchase-email).

## Troubleshooting

1. **A review was sent but does not show:** it may be waiting for approval in the reviews table, because of a moderation rule.
2. **The form does not show:** check **Who can review**. Visitors who may not review do not see the form.
