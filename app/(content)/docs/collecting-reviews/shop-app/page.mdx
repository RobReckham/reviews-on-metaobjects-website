# Shop app

Reviews stored as Shopify's standard product review metaobjects — including reviews from the Shop app — are detected and kept in sync automatically.

## Behavior

- No manual import or credentials are needed.
- Reviews are captured via Shopify webhooks as the metaobjects change.
- They appear in your dashboard and storefront app blocks after sync.

## Important

Because these reviews already live in Shopify's standard metaobjects, FiveOh reads them in place — there is nothing to connect.

## Troubleshooting

- If reviews are missing, confirm the reviews exist as standard product review metaobjects on the store.
- If updates lag, wait for sync to complete before rechecking storefront output.
