# Metaobjects & metafields

FiveOh stores everything in your Shopify store, as two kinds of Shopify data:

1. **Metafields** are extra fields on Shopify's own objects. FiveOh adds ratings, review lists, AI summaries and sales figures to your products, variants, shop, customers and orders.
2. **Metaobjects** are records of their own. Every review is one: Shopify's standard `product_review`, plus FiveOh's `review_meta` with the translations.

Your theme reads both with Liquid, like any product data. This page lists all of them. If you are new to building on FiveOh, start with [Developers](/docs/developers).
